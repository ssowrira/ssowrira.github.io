Current Work

Ongoing projects focused on medical robotics, high-fidelity simulation, and contact-aware manipulation.

Robot-Assisted FibroScan

Development of a robotic platform capable of performing vibration-controlled transient elastography (VCTE) for liver diagnostics. The system integrates robotic manipulation, force control, and probe vibration dynamics to improve measurement consistency and support future autonomous diagnostics.

Robotic Simulation and Multi-Physics Modeling

Development of simulation pipelines using MuJoCo to model robotic grasping and probe interactions. This includes generating nodal displacement data for downstream physical modeling tools and integrating multi-physics frameworks such as preCICE.

Contact-Aware Robotic Systems

Exploring methods for controlling robotic manipulators during contact-rich tasks, including impedance-based control strategies and force feedback integration.
